# Fixed scripted conversations for sensei-engine/scripts/live-eval.sh.
#
#   @conversation <id> | <description>   starts a conversation (3 to 5 learner lines)
#   @band <band>                          the player picks this starting band before the first line (optional)
#   > <text>                              a learner line
#   >* <text>                             a learner line in which the learner really tries Japanese: Sensei is expected
#                                         to record an attempt on that turn (the eval counts these for its 80% target)
#
# The lines are typed transcripts, as the speech recogniser would hand them over.

@conversation zero_water | zero-knowledge learner asks for something to drink and says the water request
> Hello! I'm a complete beginner. Can you show me something to drink?
>* Mizu o kudasai.
> Thank you! What should we do next?
>* おちゃをください。

@conversation off_topic | beginner asks two off-topic questions in the middle of the activity, then goes back to it
@band early_beginner
> Hi Sensei! I know a few words already. Can we practice asking for things?
> Quick question first: how do you say "train station" in Japanese?
> Thanks! And what about "airport"?
>* Okay, back to the game. りんごをください。

@conversation intermediate_explains | intermediate learner explains a choice in Japanese
@band intermediate
> こんにちは。今日は日本語で話したいです。何かできますか？
>* 私はお茶を選びます。水より体にいいと思うからです。
>* でも朝はコーヒーが飲みたいです。眠いですから。
> ありがとうございます。次は何をしますか？

@conversation advanced_register | advanced learner asks how a request changes with the listener
@band advanced
>* すみません、お茶をいただけますでしょうか。
> 友達に言うならどうなりますか？
>* じゃあ、お茶ちょうだい。これでいい？
> 上司に頼む場合はどうですか？

@conversation mixed_languages | learner mixes English and Japanese in the same line
> Hello! 今日はちょっと疲れていますけど I want to practice. Can we start?
>* りんごをください、please!
> What is the difference between これ and それ? ちょっとわからない。
>* Okay, じゃあ、それをください。

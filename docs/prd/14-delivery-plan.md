## 14 Delivery plan

The participant guide lists a build period of 27 October through 4 November and a submission deadline of 4 November at 9 PM EST, which is 5 November at 11 AM in Japan. Plan to finish the submission package earlier on 4 November JST. The required demo video is three minutes. \[S1\]

| **Day** | **Delivery outcome** | **Exit condition** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | BYOK voice, C# tool loop, and Quest shell. | Prove novel Japanese and English synthesis with networking off, reconnect for the Meta tool loop, and pin a local voice/runtime configuration that preserves Quest rendering and interruption. |
| 2 | Passthrough scene, ghost red panda, object catalog, and controls. | Empty-room placement, controller manipulation, and stable flat-sprite rendering work. |
| 3 | Game-master tools and task validation. | Sensei creates a scene and task through tools; a natural request invokes the correct action and gets an actual engine result. |
| 4 | Complete zero-knowledge journey and help controls. | A learner can ask a real question, request help, and reach a supported success. |
| 5 | All practice bands and sustained conversation. | Intermediate repair and advanced Japanese-only discussion work on the same scene. |
| 6 | Evidence, fresh variations, persistence, and recovery. | Assistance is tracked, restart restores progress, and stale turns cannot corrupt the scene. |
| 7 | Visual and audio polish plus content review. | Readable Japanese, clear character states, accurate launch content, and reliable audio transitions. |
| 8 | User trials, acceptance checks, and demo rehearsal. | Known failures are fixed or honestly documented; the live demonstration fits three minutes. |
| 9 | Freeze, final capture, repository, and submission package. | Public source, setup instructions, asset provenance, model configuration, and accessible video are ready before the deadline. |

Workstreams can run in parallel after day one's integration contract: Unity interaction; C# agent tools, direct Meta adapters, and local TTS integration; pedagogy and content; verification and demo. For a solo build, preserve the same order and omit P1 work. Do not postpone the live voice proof until art and lessons are finished.

### Cut order

Cut cosmetic rewards, extra object packs, hand tracking, additional explanation languages, room persistence, cloud accounts, and external product integration before reducing the required experience. Retain one complete conversational path at each starting band and a small reliable set of grounded actions.

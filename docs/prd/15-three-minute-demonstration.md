## 15 Three minute demonstration

| **Time** | **What the viewer sees** | **What it establishes** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:20 | An empty room in passthrough; ghost red panda appears; the speaker says they know no Japanese. | The product starts at zero and needs no props. |
| 0:20–0:55 | Sensei teaches a short request, answers a real question, and hands over the requested cardboard object. | Two-way voice, grounded meaning, and a useful first interaction. |
| 0:55–1:25 | A new arrangement appears with less help and the learner makes a fresh attempt. | Progressive practice and honest assistance tracking. |
| 1:25–1:55 | The demonstrator makes an unscripted supported request involving a different object, quantity, or placement. | The LLM handles language and references while the world responds. |
| 1:55–2:30 | An explicitly labeled advanced profile discusses a choice and asks a Japanese clarification or register question. | Advanced practice is available immediately; this is a separate ability demonstration. |
| 2:30–2:50 | A brief redacted tool trace, actual task outcome, and independent versus assisted evidence. | Engineering and learning claims are tied to recorded behavior. |
| 2:50–3:00 | Saved progress and another spoken exchange with Sensei. | A coherent product that can continue beyond the demo. |

The central wow moment is a beginner using Japanese to make something happen in their own surroundings, then seeing Sensei respond naturally when the conversation goes off the expected path. Show the actual headset output. Any edited waiting time or separate advanced-profile segment must be clear.

### Judging alignment

| **Criterion** | **Evidence in this product** |
| --- | --- |
| Impact | Accessible practice from zero through advanced conversation, a concrete first-session outcome, and a credible returning-learning loop. |
| Use of Meta AI tooling | Live speech understanding, unscripted dialogue, reference resolution, adaptive challenge planning, and validated actions within one experience. |
| Technical depth and execution | One-key Meta integration, on-device bilingual synthesis, a C# game-master tool loop, authoritative world state, cancellation, and assistance-aware evidence. |
| Demo | Visible consequences of Japanese speech, a genuine unexpected exchange, a fresh attempt with less help, and explicit advanced-level proof. |

The guide weights these four criteria equally and discourages simple education chatbots. The submission should demonstrate the complete learning loop and grounded actions, rather than relying on the character design alone. No win probability or unmeasured learning outcome is claimed. \[S1\]

## 6 Learner model and progression

| **Dimension** | **Evidence retained** | **Adaptation** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Vocabulary | Introduced items; recognition versus production; recent use; assistance. | Choose familiar material, a small amount of new language, and useful review. |
| Listening | Instruction length, steps, repetitions, and verified actions. | Adjust complexity, pacing, and number of simultaneous constraints. |
| Production | Meanings expressed, attempted patterns, confirmed recurring errors. | Select a useful next speaking goal and focused feedback. |
| Interaction | Clarification, follow-up questions, self-correction, and paraphrase. | Add exchanges that require conversational repair and negotiation. |
| Register | Requested social relationship and demonstrated reformulations. | Practice natural casual, neutral, or polite expression in context. |
| Support | Translation, visible text, hints, modeled answers, and demonstrations. | Fade assistance gradually without removing user control. |
| Assessment certainty | Sparse evidence, ambiguous recognition, and conflicting inputs. | Defer conclusions and request a fresh attempt. |
| Preferences | Starting band, help language, captions, pace, and correction style. | Honor explicit choices immediately. |

### Outcome categories

Every meaningful attempt is classified as independent success, success after repetition, success after a hint, success after a model answer, completed by demonstration, confirmed incorrect, or unresolved input. Store the target, assistance shown before the attempt, evidence excerpt, relevant scene revision, and engine result. Unresolved input is excluded from progression calculations.

When Sensei fulfills an independently spoken Japanese request, that can count as spoken-production evidence even though Sensei moves the object. It does not count as the learner's manual listening-comprehension response. Distinguish a requested action from Sensei demonstrating the answer, and record who performed each action.

### Assistance by learning target

| **Target** | **Assistance that prevents an independent label** |
| --- | --- |
| Listening | A visible transcript or translation of the instruction, a highlighted answer, a demonstration, or a repeated instruction; record the relevant support instead. |
| Japanese word recall | The Japanese answer, reading, romaji, or a recent audio model is supplied; the object picture or an English meaning can remain the recall cue. |
| Spoken production | A full or partial model answer, required wording, or a recent spoken demonstration supplies the target expression; an open topic or communicative goal is allowed. |
| Reading when targeted | An English translation, audio reading of the target text, or an answer highlight supplies the meaning before the learner responds. |

Independent means the relevant aid was absent; it does not mean the language was automatically proven correct. Preserve engine-verified task state and model-assessed language evidence in separate fields. A completed delivery proves the object moved, while the correctness and appropriateness of the spoken request remain a language assessment with its source and uncertainty recorded.

### Initial adaptation rules

- After three independent successes in four comparable attempts, including one fresh variation, offer one increase in complexity or one reduction in default help.

- After two confirmed difficulties on the same target, offer a simpler contrast, a demonstration, or a smaller step; do not demote unrelated abilities.

- An explicit 'too easy,' 'too hard,' 'Japanese only,' or 'explain in English' takes effect on the next response.

- Showing an answer, demonstrating a move, or doing the task for the learner cannot increase independent-production evidence.

- Revisit uncertain items during the session and in a later session; avoid immediate repetitions being the only evidence.

- Use labels such as Practiced, Used with help, and Used independently. Reserve a stronger stability label for repeated evidence across sessions.

These are initial product rules to evaluate, not a validated proficiency scale. Save the rule version with outcomes so later changes do not silently rewrite what an earlier result meant.

### Motivation

The P0 reward is a compact record of what the learner can now do, plus a short encouragement animation. A small cosmetic collection can follow after the core experience is reliable. Language levels, explanations, and free conversation are never locked behind points, streaks, payments, or cosmetic rewards.
